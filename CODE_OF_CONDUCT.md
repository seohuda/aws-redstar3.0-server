# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the project.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people
- documenting assumptions and limitations
- helping contributors reproduce and fix problems
- respecting privacy, licensing, and security boundaries

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
remarks, doxxing, deliberate publication of secrets, or intentionally posting
copyrighted installation media that you do not have permission to distribute.

## Project discussions

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the infrastructure, automation,
documentation, and operation of this repository. Political advocacy or
unrelated debate is outside the scope of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

If a situation involves a security vulnerability or sensitive information,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.
